808 CONVENTION 2027FAQ

FAQFrequently asked questions

Can't find an answer? Get in touch.

Q01When is the convention?

The 2027 808 Convention runs from Friday 5 to Sunday 7 March. See the schedule page for the full programme.

Q02Who are the guests?

The full guest list and bios are on the Guests page.

Q03How do I register?

Passes and registration details are listed on the registration page once confirmed. When registration is open, a button appears there.

Q04Do workshops need separate registration?

Yes. Lectures and workshops are booked separately from convention passes. Fees and seats are set per session, and registration closes when a session is full. Sessions are listed on the registration page.

Q05How can I contact the organisers?

Email 808conventions@gmail.com or add our LINE account: 808magicstore.

808 CONVENTION 2027GUESTS

GuestsGuest lineup

Featured guests and lecturers.

AnnouncedAnnounced

GUEST 01

Juliana Chen

茱莉安娜陳

Featured guest

Winner of the Manipulation category at FISM 1997. At this convention she presents two acts: her FISM-winning stage routine, and a full close-up show, newly developed and performed in public for the first time.

GUEST 02

Armando Lucero

Featured guest

Renowned for masterful card and coin magic and refined physical performance, he blends advanced sleight of hand, audience psychology and dramatic tension. Beyond performing, he teaches and lectures widely on technique, creativity and the art of magic. At this convention he performs a full show.

GUEST 03

Ryusei Kamiguchi

上口龍生

Special guestFeatured guest

A professional magician from Japan specialising in sleight of hand, stage illusion and wazuma, the traditional Japanese art of magic, and a successor to the late master Haruo Shimada. He has performed at the Sydney Opera House and appears on Japanese television and at international magic events, working to preserve traditional Japanese magic by uniting classical technique with stagecraft. At this convention he presents two acts.

GUEST 04

Taylor Morgan

泰勒摩根

Featured guest

A British magician and illusionist with more than 2,500 large-scale stage performances across theatres in the UK and Europe. He headlined the resident show at The Magic Circle for 30 consecutive weeks and has represented the UK at FISM three times. His performances combine visual impact with emotional storytelling.

GUEST 05

Rebecca Foyle

雷貝卡·佛伊爾

Featured guest

A British stage magician and variety performer with a background in dance, combining visual magic, choreography and circus skills. With Taylor Morgan she co-starred in Chasing Miracles at FRINGE WORLD in Australia and at Edinburgh's MagicFest. Her signature acts include a flying LED cane and fire performance.
